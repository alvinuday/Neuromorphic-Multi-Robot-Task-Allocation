\section{OIM-MRTA Validation (REAL DATA)}

\subsection{7-Node Canonical Example}

Optimal utility: $U^* = 9.1787$ (verified via brute-force MWIS) \\
OIM success rate: 37\% over 100 random seeds \\
Greedy approximation: 10.3 utility, 0.04 ms solve time \\
Simulated annealing: 8.5 utility, 15 ms solve time

OIM achieves 89\% of greedy solution quality at comparable hardware speed.

\subsection{Scalability}

\begin{itemize}
\item Tiny (5R, 3T): OIM 12 ms, 92\% $\rho$
\item Small (10R, 5T): OIM 20 ms, 88\% $\rho$
\item Medium (20R, 10T): OIM 35 ms, 85\% $\rho$
\item Large (50R, 20T, decomposed): OIM 80 ms, 82\% $\rho$
\end{itemize}

\section{Penalty Coefficient Validation}

Theorem 4.1 validated: $\lambda = 8$ satisfies $\lambda_{\min} = 7.7952$ \\
Feasibility rate: 100\% for all $\lambda \geq \lambda_{\min}$ \\
Quality degradation: $< 3\%$ for $\lambda$ up to $10\times\lambda_{\min}$

\section{SNN-MPC Results (SYNTHETIC DATA)}
