\section{Comprehensive Solver Comparison}

This section presents rigorous benchmarking of OIM against classical MWIS solvers on synthetic instances.

\subsection{Benchmark Setup}

We generated 48 diverse MRTA instances across:
\begin{itemize}
\item Problem scales: 5-robot/3-task to 50-robot/20-task scenarios
\item Sparsity levels: sparse, medium, dense conflict graphs
\item Utility distributions: uniform, skewed, power-law, exponential, bimodal
\end{itemize}

Translated to MWIS problems: 222--255 nodes, 11,000--15,000 edges per instance.

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{vs Branch \& Bound}: 65$\times$ faster (0.14 ms vs 9.1 ms), 9$\times$ lower energy
\item \textbf{vs Local Search}: 74$\times$ faster (0.14 ms vs 10.4 ms), 10$\times$ lower energy

\end{itemize}

OIM is the primary platform meeting hard real-time constraints for autonomous robotics without sacrificing energy efficiency.

\subsubsection{Note on Neuromorphic Alternatives}

While Intel Loihi \cite{Davies2018} represents state-of-the-art in spiking neural network processors, its 1 MHz neuromorphic clock results in multi-hundred-millisecond latencies for iterative algorithms---incompatible with 10--20 ms real-time robotics constraints. OIM's injection-locking mechanism enables convergence in nanosecond-timescale physical processes, rather than digital simulation, providing orders of magnitude speedup for combinatorial optimization.
