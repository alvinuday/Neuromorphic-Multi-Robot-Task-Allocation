\section{4-Layer Bits-to-Atoms Stack}

\subsection{Layer 1: Physical World}

Multi-robot teams with sensors and actuators. Real-time constraints (10--20 ms).

\subsection{Layer 2: Neuromorphic Hardware}

OIM (oscillator Ising machines) for combinatorial optimization. SNNs for iterative control. Event-driven, low power, CMOS-compatible.

\subsection{Layer 3: Mathematical Encoding}

MRTA to MWIS to QUBO. Robot dynamics to QP. Problem-independent layer.

\subsection{Layer 4: Problem Formulation}

Coalition sizes, task requirements, robot capabilities. Specific to application domain.

\section{Trade-off Space}

Classical approaches (CPU):
\begin{itemize}
\item Exact MWIS: exponential time
\item OSQP MPC: millisecond latency
\item Energy: watts
\end{itemize}

Neuromorphic approaches:
\begin{itemize}
\item OIM: polynomial hardware time, approximate quality
\item SNN-MPC: event-driven, low energy
\item Latency: hardware-limited
\end{itemize}

OIM and SNN excel when latency and energy are critical constraints.
