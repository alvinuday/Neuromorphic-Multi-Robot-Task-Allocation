        \chapter*{On AI Assistance}

        This thesis was authored by the author with AI assistance accelerating key research phases. Large language models (Claude) were deployed to: (1) rapidly prototype mathematical formulations and verify derivations against literature, (2) conduct deep literature synthesis across neuromorphic platforms, Ising machines, and robot control, (3) generate publication-quality figures from experimental data, and (4) refine written exposition for clarity and accessibility.

        Specifically: (1) Mathematical theorems and lemmas are the author's original work; AI verified proofs for logical consistency and helped restructure for readability. (2) Experimental code (Python, LaTeX) was authored by the author; AI accelerated testing, debugging, and documentation. (3) Figures were designed by the author and rendered by AI tools (Plotly, matplotlib); AI handled layout and visual refinement. (4) Writing was authored by the author; AI condensed verbose sections, improved clarity, and enhanced narrative flow without changing technical substance.

        No experimental results, proofs, or novel algorithms were generated by AI. All research contributions, conceptual insights, and technical claims are the author's. AI's role was accelerating research velocity—reducing prototyping time, deepening literature coverage, and polishing presentation—enabling faster iteration and more rigorous exposition.
