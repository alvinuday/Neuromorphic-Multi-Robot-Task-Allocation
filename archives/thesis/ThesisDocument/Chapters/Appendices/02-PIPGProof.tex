\chapter{PIPG Algorithm and Convergence}
\label{app:pipg}

\section{Algorithm Statement}

Proportional-Integral Projected Gradient (PIPG) for constrained QP:
\[
\min_x \frac{1}{2} x^T Q x + p^T x \quad \text{s.t.} \quad Ax \leq b
\]

Iteration $k$:
\begin{align}
x^{k+1} &= \text{Proj}_C(x^k - \alpha_P (Q x^k + p)) \\
\alpha_P &= 2 / (1 + \kappa(Q))
\end{align}

where $\text{Proj}_C$ projects onto feasible set $C = \{x : Ax \leq b\}$ and $\kappa(Q)$ is the condition number.

\section{Convergence Rate}

For strongly convex $Q$ with $\lambda_{\min}(Q) = \lambda$, PIPG converges at rate:
\[
\|x^k - x^*\| \leq \rho^k \|x^0 - x^*\|, \quad \rho = \frac{\kappa - 1}{\kappa + 1}
\]

For well-conditioned problems ($\kappa \approx 100$), $\rho \approx 0.98$, meaning convergence in $\approx 50$ iterations.
